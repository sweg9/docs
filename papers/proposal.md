# Proposal

**Vision-Based Recognition and Actuation of Unconnected Home Controls.** Geonhak Lee, Jemin An, Jiwon Han, Hyunseo Choi. Department of Information Systems, Hanyang University.

This paper is the reference for every page in this wiki. When a page and the paper disagree, the paper wins.

::: warning One change since the paper
The paper sends crops to a cloud VLM to read controls, infer intent and check results. The team replaced it with local models on the hub (2026-11-17), so the system calls no model API. See [Models](/product/models). Everything else follows the paper.
:::
